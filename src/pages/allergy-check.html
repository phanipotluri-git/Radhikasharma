Two-minute check

Is this allergy, or something else?

Seven questions about the pattern of your symptoms — not a diagnosis, and not a substitute for being examined. It tells you whether what you are describing is the shape of allergy, and therefore whether testing is likely to be worth your time.

Answered entirely in your browser. Nothing is recorded, sent or stored — not by this website, and not by anyone else.

What this is

A sorting tool, not a diagnosis

No questionnaire can diagnose allergy. Only a history taken by a doctor, read together with a test, can do that — and a positive test on its own is not a diagnosis either.

What this can do is tell you whether your symptoms follow the pattern allergy usually takes. That is a genuinely useful thing to know before spending money on testing.

If you are having trouble breathing now, this is the wrong page — go to the nearest emergency department.
