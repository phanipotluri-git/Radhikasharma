Before you come

Two things worth knowing

01

Stop antihistamines five days before allergy testing

They suppress the skin response and make the test unreadable — including the ones hidden in cold and cough medicines. Everything else continues as normal. Full instructions are on the first visit page.

02

Send earlier reports ahead of time

Previous test results, prescriptions and scans on WhatsApp before your appointment mean more of the visit is spent on you and less on paperwork.

Urgent symptoms

This form is for routine appointments and is not monitored continuously. If you are severely breathless, your reliever inhaler is not working, your lips or face are swelling, or you are having a severe allergic reaction, do not wait for a reply — use an adrenaline auto-injector if you have been prescribed one, and go to the nearest emergency department or call for an ambulance.
