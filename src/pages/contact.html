Practical

Before you book

Do I need a referral?

No. You can book directly on WhatsApp or by phone. If another doctor has been treating this, bring their notes — and with your permission we will write back to them.

What should I do before an allergy testing appointment?

Stop antihistamines for five days, including the ones hidden in cold and cough medicines. Continue everything else. The full instructions are on the first visit page.

What does it cost?

Consultation and test charges are explained when you book, and nothing is ordered without being discussed with you first. Cash, UPI and cards are accepted.

Do you see children?

Yes. Paediatric allergy is a significant part of the practice — eczema, recurrent wheeze, food reactions and allergic rhinitis in children are all seen here.

Can I send reports in advance?

Please do. Sending previous test results, prescriptions and scans on WhatsApp before your appointment means more of the visit is spent on you and less on paperwork.
